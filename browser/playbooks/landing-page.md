# Landing page

A landing page has one job: get one kind of visitor to take one action. Every section either moves them toward it or gets out of the way.

## Above the fold

What a visitor sees without scrolling decides whether they scroll. In order of importance:

1. **Headline** — the outcome, in the visitor's words, ≤ 8 words, specific. Formula: *[Outcome] for [who] without [pain]* or *[Verb] your [thing] in [time]*. "Invoices paid twice as fast" beats "The modern invoicing platform".
2. **Subhead** — how, and for whom, ≤ 20 words. The headline promises; the subhead makes it believable.
3. **Primary CTA** — one button, verb + value ("Start free trial", "Get the template"), 44px tall or more, the only filled button in the viewport, with a reassurance under it ("No card needed · 2-minute setup").
4. **The product in use** — a real screenshot, short video or illustration of the result, not abstract shapes.
5. **A proof strip** — logos, a rating, a number ("Trusted by 12,000 teams") directly under the hero.

Navigation stays minimal (logo, 3–5 links, the same CTA). Remove anything that competes with the CTA: carousels, pop-ups on load, two hero buttons of equal weight.

## Section order

Hero → proof strip → problem and three benefits → how it works (three steps) → features grouped by job → testimonials (name, face or company, a specific result) → pricing → FAQ (the objections) → final CTA.

Each section answers the next question in the visitor's head: *What is it? Is it for me? Does it work? How? Can I trust it? What does it cost? What if…? OK, how do I start?*

## Copy rules

- Benefits before features; each feature named by the job it does ("Reminders that chase late payers for you").
- Specific numbers ("saves 4 hours a week", "2-minute setup"); drop "innovative, seamless, powerful, all-in-one".
- One CTA, the same words everywhere it repeats. Secondary CTAs ("Watch demo") are text links.
- Headlines in sentence case, ≤ 8 words; paragraphs ≤ 3 lines on desktop.

## Design rules

- Container 1140–1200px; section padding 96px desktop / 48px mobile; alternate backgrounds (white / tinted) to mark rhythm.
- Three columns max; benefit cards with an icon, a 4-word title and a 1–2 line description.
- One accent for CTAs; proof and testimonials in neutral styling so the accent stays with the action.
- Pricing: three tiers, the recommended one visually lifted (border, badge "Most popular"), monthly/annual toggle, the per-seat basis explicit, the CTA on every tier.
- Mobile: single column, CTA within thumb reach, a sticky bottom CTA after the hero scrolls away; hero image ≤ 200 KB.

## Trust

Logos of customers, counts, a money-back guarantee near the price, security badges near payment, a real person's photo in testimonials, a visible contact. Trust elements sit where doubt arises: near the price and the form.

## Scoring rubric

Score each 0–2 (0 missing, 1 present but weak, 2 strong). A page under 12/20 has a conversion problem in the structure, not the colours.

| Section | 2 points when |
| --- | --- |
| Headline | outcome-led, specific, ≤ 8 words |
| Subhead | how + who, ≤ 20 words |
| Primary CTA | one, verb + value, reassurance, ≥ 44px, above the fold |
| Product shown | real product, the result visible |
| Proof strip | logos / rating / number near the hero |
| Benefits | three, job-named, specific |
| How it works | three steps, each one line |
| Testimonials | named, specific result |
| Pricing | 3 tiers, recommended one lifted, basis clear |
| Objections | FAQ or guarantee answers the top 5 doubts |

## Mocking up a change

With the elements tool, show instead of tell: add a proof strip under the hero, replace the hero headline text with the proposed one, add a sticky CTA bar, or hide the second hero button — then screenshot both versions. Never change prices, legal text or anything that would mislead if left on the page; the mock-ups are for the user's eyes and are removable.

## Checklist

- [ ] One action, one CTA, repeated with the same words
- [ ] Headline = outcome; subhead = how + who
- [ ] Proof near the hero and near the price
- [ ] Sections in the question order; each answers one doubt
- [ ] Pricing with a lifted recommendation and a clear basis
- [ ] Mobile: single column, reachable CTA, light hero image
